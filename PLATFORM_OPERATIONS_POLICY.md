# HelpFundMe Internal Operations Policy

Version: 2026-09-20. Owner: Forgex Company Limited. This operational draft must be reviewed by qualified Ghanaian counsel and the company’s compliance lead before launch.

## Campaign approval and rejection

- No campaign may accept donations unless its status is `approved` and `isActive` is true.
- A reviewer must open the full campaign, review applicant identity, beneficiary relationship, consent, budget, and category evidence, and record notes before deciding.
- Medical cases require an invoice/bill or treatment evidence and patient/authorized-representative consent. Minors require guardian consent.
- Education cases require a school invoice/fee statement, student identifier, relationship/authority, and school confirmation where appropriate.
- Emergency cases require location, photos, damage/cost evidence, and an authority, organization, or witness report where available.
- Community/charity projects require a plan, budget, authorization, and registration details where applicable.
- Approval means defined checks were completed; it is not a guarantee. Reject or request more information where material claims cannot be reasonably corroborated.

## Fraud and suspicious activity escalation

- Keep reports confidential and restrict access to assigned reviewers.
- Preserve submissions, decision history, payment references, account activity, and communications.
- Suspend fundraising and disbursement promptly where donor harm may continue.
- Independently contact issuing organizations using independently sourced contact details; do not rely only on contact details inside uploaded evidence.
- Give the affected user a reasonable chance to respond unless this risks safety, evidence, or a lawful investigation.
- Do not publicly label a person fraudulent before a completed review and appropriate legal advice.

## Complaints and appeals

- Acknowledge complaints, assign a case owner, identify conflicts, and provide a reasoned written outcome.
- Permit one appeal with new evidence or a specific alleged review error; assign a reviewer who did not make the original decision where practicable.

## Refunds and donor disputes

- Confirm transaction status and available funds before promising a refund.
- Coordinate with Paystack and financial partners; preserve charge, webhook, receipt, campaign, and communication records.
- Consider misrepresentation, duplicate/unauthorized payment, campaign cancellation, unattainable purpose, and regulatory/payment-provider obligations.
- Notify affected donors when a campaign is suspended and provide neutral status information without premature accusations.

## Data retention and deletion

- Apply role-based access and short-lived signed links to identity, medical, education, and other private evidence.
- Keep verification and financial records only for documented operational, fraud-prevention, dispute, and legal periods. Record holds that prevent deletion.
- Log access and administrative decisions. Fulfil lawful access/correction/deletion requests after verifying the requester and checking retention duties.

## Security incidents

- Contain access, preserve evidence, rotate affected credentials, assess scope, and notify management, processors, users, regulators, and CERT-GH where legally required.
- Document the timeline, decisions, notifications, recovery, and corrective actions. Never conceal a material incident.
